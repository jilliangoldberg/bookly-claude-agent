# Bookly policies
One section per policy, headed `## [id] Title`. Dewey may only answer policy questions from these.

## [ship-01] Shipping speeds and costs
Orders ship within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders of $35 or more; otherwise it costs $4.99. Expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs $9.99.

## [ship-02] Where we ship
Bookly ships to addresses in the United States and Canada only. We don't ship to other countries yet. Shipping to Canada uses the same speeds and prices as the US.

## [ship-03] Tracking and lost packages
Every shipped order gets a carrier tracking number, emailed when it ships and shown in your order details. If tracking hasn't moved for 5 or more business days past the estimated delivery date, a support teammate will open a lost-package claim with the carrier.

## [ret-01] Return window and condition
Physical books can be returned within 30 days of delivery, in like-new condition. Day 30 after delivery is the last day to start a return.

## [ret-02] Refund methods and timing
For a change-of-mind return, you can get a refund to your original payment minus a $4.99 return-label fee, or the full amount as store credit. Refunds to the original payment arrive 5-7 business days after the return reaches us. Store credit is added as soon as the return arrives.

## [ret-03] Damaged or wrong items
If a book arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, tell us within 30 days of delivery. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund, and we'll send a prepaid return label with no fee.

## [ret-04] Ebooks and digital purchases
Ebooks can be refunded within 7 days of purchase, but only if they were never downloaded. Once an ebook has been downloaded it can't be refunded.

## [ord-01] Preorders
Preorders ship on the book's release date, and you're charged when the order ships. Preorders can't be returned before delivery; to cancel a preorder, a support teammate can help.

## [ord-02] Order changes and cancellations
Cancelling an order or changing its shipping address is handled by our support team, not online. Contact support as soon as possible; once an order has shipped it can't be changed.

## [pay-01] Accepted payment methods
Bookly accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Bookly gift cards. We never ask for your full card number or password in chat.

## [pm-01] Price matching
Bookly doesn't offer price matching or price adjustments with other stores or after purchase.

## [acct-01] Password reset
You can reset your password by requesting a reset link sent to your account email. The link expires after 30 minutes. Bookly staff will never ask for your password.
